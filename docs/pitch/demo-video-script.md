# Chhat — 90-Second Demo Video Script

**Format:** screen recording of the Chhat web app + voiceover (English, with Urdu lines where marked).
**Recording setup:** 1920x1080, app open full-screen, DEMO badge visible. Record each shot separately, then assemble.
**Test address (use exactly):** `House 123, Street 4, Block C, Nishat Colony, Lahore`

---

## SHOT 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)

**Visual:** Chhat landing page. Cursor moves to the address box.
**On-screen text overlay:** "Electricity: Rs 47.69/unit"

**EN:** "Electricity in Lahore costs 47 rupees 69 paisa a unit, and the light goes out every single day. Everyone wants solar. But getting a quote takes a week, a salesman, and a site visit. Watch this."

**UR:** "Lahore mein bijli 47 rupay 69 paisay per unit hai, aur light roz jaati hai. Solar sab chahtay hain, lekin quote lenay mein hafta lag jaata hai. Yeh dekhiye."

**Action:** Type the address slowly, clearly. 10 seconds.

---

## SHOT 2 — The parser (0:10–0:25)

**Visual:** Click **Analyze my roof**. Parsed-field chips pop in one by one: House 123 / Street 4 / Block C / Nishat Colony / Lahore.

**EN:** "First, our own trained AI reads the address. Pakistani addresses are messy — H-hash-45, Makan 89 Gali 2 — so we trained our own parser on ten thousand of them. It never guesses; it understands."

**UR:** "Pehle hamari apni trained AI address parhti hai. Pakistani address ultay-seedhay hotay hain, is liye hum ne apna parser khud train kiya hai."

**Action:** Let each chip appear. Pause on the chips for 3 seconds.

---

## SHOT 3 — The pin (0:25–0:35)

**Visual:** Map view. Drag the pin slightly onto the rooftop. Click **Confirm location**.

**EN:** "Now I drop the pin on my exact roof. Two taps — no confusion, no wrong house."

**UR:** "Ab mein pin apni chhat par lagata hoon. Bas do tap — na ghalat ghar, na confusion."

---

## SHOT 4 — The roof AI (0:35–0:55)

**Visual:** Roof schematic appears. The AI roof mask draws itself, then solar panels pop on one by one around the water-tank obstruction.

**EN:** "And there is my roof — read by our own rooftop AI, trained from scratch on Lahore imagery. Watch it place eighteen panels around the water tank. If it is unsure, it says so — it never hallucinates a roof."

**UR:** "Aur yeh rahi meri chhat — hamari apni AI ne parhi hai. Dekhiye, paani wali tanki ke ird-gird athara panel lag gaye."

**Action:** Let panels finish popping (about 8 seconds). Do not rush this shot.

---

## SHOT 5 — The numbers (0:55–1:15)

**Visual:** Chhat Score dial pulses to 77. KPI cards fade in: 9.9 kW · 18 panels · Rs 1,424,500 · payback 2.4 years.

**EN:** "Chhat Score: seventy-seven out of a hundred. Nine point nine kilowatts. Fourteen lakh twenty-four thousand rupees. Payback in two point four years — sized for the new net-billing rules, where self-use beats export."

**UR:** "Chhat Score: sattar. System: nau kilowatt. Qeemat: chauda lakh. Do saal mein paisay wapas — naye net-billing qawaneen ke hisaab se."

---

## SHOT 6 — The closer (1:15–1:30)

**Visual:** Back to the address box, empty and blinking. Chhat logo.

**EN:** "Type any address in Pakistan. Sixty seconds. No salesman, no site visit. That is Chhat — your roof, read by AI."

**UR:** "Pakistan ka koi bhi address likhiye. Saath second. Na salesman, na visit. Yeh hai Chhat — AI aapki chhat parhay."

**On-screen text overlay:** "CHHAT · AI rooftop solar intelligence for Pakistan"

---

## Production notes

- **Honesty:** the red DEMO badge must be visible in every shot. If asked, say: "Demo roof data today — the pipeline is identical with the Solar API key."
- **Pacing:** shots 4 and 5 are the money shots. Give them room to breathe.
- **Audio:** record narration after the screen capture, in a quiet room. Keep Urdu lines warm and conversational, not formal.
- **Thumbnail:** see `docs/pitch/thumbnail.jpg` — dark green solar theme, rooftop with panels, "CHHAT" title.
- **Export:** 1080p, H.264, under 50 MB for the application form upload.
